## r26.0 information architecture

The repository now uses three audience layers:

- consumer guidance;
- developer implementation and roadmap;
- research and evidence.

Canonical shared facts live under `docs/reference/`. Historical numbered tests,
findings, experiments, runbooks, methodology, evidence, and research releases
remain at their established paths. Former `docs/development/`,
`docs/references/`, and phone-compatibility paths are retained as explicit
forwarders.
