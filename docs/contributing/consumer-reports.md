## Include

- exact product family without serial number;
- phone model and region;
- phone OS and Hi Rokid version;
- glasses firmware;
- feature or problem tested;
- exact steps;
- result and repeatability;
- whether the claim is official, observed, inferred, or unverified.

## Exclude

- device serials and Bluetooth addresses;
- account IDs and authorization values;
- precise location;
- private conversations, photos, or recordings;
- raw logs or captures.

Use the consumer issue templates where available. A compatibility report proves
only the stated phone, app, firmware, region, and feature combination.
