## Required structure

1. Purpose and controlled variable.
2. Exact device, phone, app, and firmware scope.
3. Preconditions and recovery path.
4. Procedure.
5. Acceptance and rejection rules.
6. Evidence completeness.
7. Sanitized result.
8. Limitations and prohibited interpretations.

## Safety requirements

- begin with read-only discovery;
- do not replay captured commands without an approved protocol gate;
- do not flash without a proven restoration route;
- keep raw evidence private;
- preserve device state and document rollback;
- distinguish a transport result from an application-protocol result.
