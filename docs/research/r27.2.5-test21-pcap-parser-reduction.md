# R27.2.5 — Test 21 PCAP parser preservation and implementation reduction

R27.2.5 preserves the exact historical `r3.3.3` and `r3.3.3.1` PCAP parser implementations before replacing their live historical paths with compatibility shims backed by the canonical `rokid-research network parse-pcap` implementation.

## Safety and provenance

- Both historical parser source hashes from R27.2.4 must match before mutation.
- The original bytes are archived privately and reverified before compatibility replacement.
- Historical filenames remain available; no repository path is deleted.
- The shims preserve the import-level API exercised by the historical regression suites.
- Source-contract snapshots that referenced the historical parser bytes are migrated to exact shim identities.
- No device, privileged, or network operation is performed by the R27.2.5 qualification harness.

## Equivalence boundary

The archived originals and compatibility shims are exercised on the same five positive and two negative synthetic cases used by R27.2.4. Successful cases require identical stdout and identical private output bytes. Unsupported PCAP magic must remain a rejecting negative case.

After acceptance, the two parser implementations count as canonicalized while the historical call paths remain compatibility entry points.
