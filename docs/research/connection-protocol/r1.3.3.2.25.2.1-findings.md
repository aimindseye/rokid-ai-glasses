# r1.3.3.2.25.2.1 — Power-State Differential BLE Identity Attribution

## Purpose

r25.2.1 resolves the blocker exposed during r25.2 qualification: the bonded
Classic identity was not observed under the same pseudonym in BLE scan results,
no scanned identity advertised service `0x9100`, and many strong unnamed devices
were present. Selecting an unnamed device by RSSI alone would not be a safe or
reproducible attribution method.

## Capture design

The Android client records three ordered, bounded phases while Hi Rokid is
disabled and stopped:

| Phase | Duration | Operator state |
|---|---:|---|
| `off_baseline` | 20 s | Glasses powered off |
| `power_on_transition` | 30 s | Power glasses on immediately before phase start |
| `on_steady` | 30 s | Glasses remain powered on |

Each phase automatically stops. A second start while a phase is active is
rejected locally and never reaches `BluetoothLeScanner.startScan()`.

## Private advertisement evidence

For each callback the client stores only bounded, privacy-reduced identity
material:

- per-run pseudonymous device ID;
- phase and elapsed time;
- RSSI and connectability;
- advertised service UUIDs;
- manufacturer company IDs, payload lengths, and payload SHA-256 values;
- service-data UUIDs, payload lengths, and payload SHA-256 values;
- raw advertisement-record length and SHA-256, but not the bytes;
- exact-payload and structure-only fingerprint SHA-256 values;
- device-name presence, length, and SHA-256, but not the name.

The public result excludes pseudonymous device IDs and all fingerprint hashes.

## Clustering and attribution gate

The private analyzer groups observations by exact payload fingerprint and by a
structure fingerprint that excludes rotating payload content. The latter can
cluster multiple private-address identities with the same advertisement shape.

A structure cluster qualifies only when all of the following are true:

1. absent from the glasses-off baseline;
2. observed at least five times during power-on transition;
3. observed at least five times during steady-on capture;
4. first observed within 15 seconds of transition start;
5. repeatedly connectable;
6. contains manufacturer, service-data, or advertised-service identity material;
7. reaches usable signal strength of at least -80 dBm.

Attribution closes only when exactly one cluster qualifies and its score margin
is at least 20 points. Otherwise the bounded capture is accepted as unresolved;
no device is selected automatically.

## Safety boundary

The r25.2.1 build exposes no GATT or RFCOMM control. It performs no
characteristic or descriptor access, obtains no socket stream, and sends or
reads no application payload. The connection-only r25.2 work resumes only after
a unique power-correlated BLE cluster is accepted.
