# r1.3.3.2.25.3.1.2 — target-pair-scoped RFCOMM error qualification and offline salvage

## Purpose

Reanalyze the accepted physical r25.3.1.1 stock-toggle capture without another
device run. Dynamic RFCOMM CIDs are discovered from the rolling snoop, but only
parse errors on handle/CID pairs that yield DLCI 6 frames block target-channel
qualification.

## Real-capture basis

The completed capture proves four stock semantic transitions, final-state
restoration, a usable read-only control channel, complete active HCI coverage,
zero drops, zero truncations, eight DLCI 6 frames, and seven payload-bearing
frames. Two parser errors were localized to a different CID from both target
pairs and outside all measured action and baseline windows.

## Qualification rule

- Structural btsnoop errors remain blocking.
- Drops and record truncation remain blocking.
- A target handle must remain unique.
- At least one DLCI 6 frame must exist.
- RFCOMM errors on target handle/CID pairs remain blocking.
- RFCOMM errors on non-target pairs are retained privately and excluded from
  target qualification.

## Boundary

This phase is host-only. It performs no ADB action, no stock toggle, no custom
RFCOMM transmission, no replay, no fastboot operation, and no partition access.
Raw payload bytes remain private.
