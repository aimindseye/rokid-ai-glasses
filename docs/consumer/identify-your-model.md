## The product covered here

This wiki covers **Rokid AI Glasses Style**, the display-free consumer model.
Its primary output is audio through open-ear speakers and information shown in
the phone companion app.

## Do not confuse it with display-equipped models

| Characteristic | Style covered by this wiki | Display-equipped Rokid products |
|---|---|---|
| In-lens display | None | Present on supported models |
| Main interaction | Voice, audio, camera, phone app | Visual UI plus model-specific controls |
| Display SDK examples | Generally not applicable | May be applicable to the exact model |
| Community APK compatibility | Must be qualified separately | Often the intended target |

A community project mentioning Rokid, YodaOS, CXR, AR rendering, touchpads, or
Micro-LED output is not automatically compatible with Style.

## Before following instructions

Confirm all of the following:

- the product name shown in the app or packaging;
- the country or region in which it was sold;
- the current Hi Rokid version;
- the glasses firmware version;
- the phone model and OS version.

Do not publish the device serial number, account identifier, or Bluetooth
address when asking for help.
